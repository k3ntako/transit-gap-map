Choropleth

0 counts residents equally. Turning it up weights car-free and lower-income households more heavily — which lowers agreement with the corridors agencies have proposed, measured, so the default is 0.

The same tracts as the score above, grouped into contiguous blocks and shaded by how many people the whole block holds. A rail line serves a strip of neighborhoods, so a block is the unit a project could act on — and a lone tract ringed by well-served ones is a bus-stop problem, a park, or a measurement artifact. Blocks stop at water: these polygons exclude it, so nothing touches across the East River and no bridge counts as a walk.

One center per borough, then the stations adding the most jobs no other center reaches. Picked from where the jobs are, not from ridership — which is circular, because the busiest stations are the radial ones.
